Bridging the Macro and the Micro

Telelink.fr is a technical exploration into the mechanics of connectivity. We believe that understanding how data moves—whether across a multi-kilometer radio link between telecom towers or through a local Kubernetes cluster in a home rack—is the key to mastering modern networking. This site serves as both a documentation portal for telecommunications infrastructure and a window into lxhome, an enterprise-grade private lab environment.

The Vision: Infrastructure as Code

At the heart of this project is the philosophy of Infrastructure as Code (IaC). We strive to demystify complex systems by documenting them with clarity, precision, and open-source transparency. Whether it is analyzing the structural evolution of cellular masts or deploying a self-healing microservices architecture via Ansible and CI/CD pipelines, our goal is to prove that robust, high-availability systems can be built and managed through disciplined, automated workflows.

About the Lab: lxhome

lxhome is not just a collection of servers; it is a sandbox for engineering excellence. Driven by a passion for DevOps, virtualization, and advanced network security, the lab functions as a "digital twin" of a modern data center. By utilizing tools like Proxmox, Kubernetes, and automated deployment scripts, we continuously refine how services are hosted, secured, and scaled. All configurations are developed in the open—you can follow our progress and contribute to our codebase at our GitHub repository.

Why Telelink?

Telecommunications and Home Lab engineering are two sides of the same coin: Connectivity. While one focuses on the massive physical networks that unite cities, the other focuses on the micro-networks that power personal and professional innovation. Telelink.fr exists to bridge these two scales, providing insights for enthusiasts, engineers, and anyone curious about how the systems behind our digital lives are built.
